Cookies Policy

Last updated: January 2026

SHAHK (“we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on https://shahk.co.uk. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding their use.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as preferences or login details, to improve your browsing experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly (e.g., navigation, security).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website. We use Google Analytics for this purpose.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings for a more personalised experience.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements. We currently do not use third-party marketing cookies.

3. How We Use Cookies

  • To analyse website traffic and usage patterns.
  • To improve website functionality and user experience.
  • To remember your preferences and choices on the website.
  • To ensure website security and prevent fraud.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services, such as Google Analytics. These third parties may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5. Your Cookie Choices

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing cookies. Please note that some parts of the website may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

For Google Analytics, you can opt out of tracking here: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

6. Changes to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
